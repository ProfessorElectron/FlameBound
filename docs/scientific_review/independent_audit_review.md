# Independent PSI Audit Review

**Review date:** 2026-11-15
**Scope:** PSI-26 BASS, PSI-25 BASS-II, PSI-98 SAFFIRE-I, PSI-99 SAFFIRE-II, PSI-100 SAFFIRE-III.
**Method:** Independent review of the repository's source inventory, experimental tables, existing file-level and row audits, and supporting files present in `data/raw/nasa_psi/`. No external data were downloaded. The original source files were not modified.

## Executive finding

The initial audit is directionally conservative and correctly keeps the harmonised table empty. It is an initial structural/source audit, not an independent verification of experiment-level facts: the BASS local-only workbook was deliberately excluded from content review, many BASS engineering drawings are image-only, and none of the row-level outcomes were crosswalked to primary narratives or payloads. Candidate table rows must not be reported as confirmed experiments. The full audit report is in [`../data_audit_report.md`](../data_audit_report.md); this review adds modelling, schema, and implementation scrutiny.

Labels: **VERIFIED** = supported by a cited local source; **DERIVED** = calculated from verified inputs; **MODELLED** = generated by an explicitly defined model; **INFERRED** = reasoned but not established; **UNVERIFIED** = unresolved; **MISSING** = unavailable locally.

## PSI-26 — BASS

**What the files represent.** `PSI-26_Experimental table_BASS.csv` is an identifier/material register with 121 keyed rows plus one soot-cleaning annotation; it does not itself provide the physical time series or structured outcomes. `BASS test matrix.xlsx`, sheet `as run matrix`, is a richer run/matrix view with 130 keyed body rows. The `2012` and `2013` sheets are alternative year views; they are not additive test sets. The data-availability sheets are reconciliation lists, not tests. These counts and the 121-key match plus nine unmatched primary-matrix keys are **VERIFIED as table structure/crosswalk results**, not confirmed unique experiments. See `docs/data_audit_report.md`, “BASS — PSI-26,” and the named CSV/workbook sheets.

**Repeated records and grain.** A composite of `As Run Test #`, `Test #`, and `Sample #` links the 121 PSI CSV identifiers to the primary matrix; that does not prove one row equals one independent burn. Year sheets repeat records as alternate views. The exact test/run/sample relationship and the nine extra keys remain **UNVERIFIED**. Resolve with a source-owner definition of each ID and compare date/time/material/observations against source run logs or video; do not add year-sheet rows to counts.

**Variables.** `O2 % by volume` in the as-run matrix includes `0.221`-scale values. The header alone does not establish whether this is fractional composition or percent-number notation (**UNVERIFIED**). Do not divide or multiply by 100. The `pressure, atm` field lacks a stated absolute/gauge basis (**UNVERIFIED**); do not convert to absolute kPa until a procedure/instrument definition confirms basis. `Air display`/`Fan display` may contain sequences or settings; a calibrated, run-resolved value and reference location are not yet established. SRD_BASS pp. 4–9 describe planned apparatus, materials, oxygen checks, flow, and instrumentation; p. 10 is a planned matrix, not evidence of completed tests. `DLD_BASS.pdf` p. 1 identifies year-organized data and image/video collections. The as-run workbook's observation prose is not a reviewed outcome code.

**Evidence needed.** Obtain the field definitions/calibration records and the actual measurement convention for O2 and pressure; inspect run-level logs/video; reconcile the nine matrix-only keys against the PSI export; obtain an independent second review. The 2013 paper `US National Meeting-070HE-0218-Final.docx` describes study-level opposed/concurrent flame behavior but does not map aggregate conclusions to each table row.

## PSI-25 — BASS-II

**What the files represent.** The PSI CSV contains 129 data rows and 129 distinct `Test #` strings distributed across six PI labels: Bhattacharjee 22, Ferkul 4, Fernandez-Pello 27, Miller 20, Olson 27, and T'ien 29. This is a **VERIFIED description of the CSV**, not proof of 129 independent burns. Fan/air display fields and observations are not an experiment-level outcome crosswalk. NASA/TM-20210011385 printed pp. 3–6 describes the apparatus and reported measurements; Appendix A pp. 103–113 contains PI-specific matrices and observations.

**126-versus-129 discrepancy.** The TM summary on printed p. 3 says 126 tests and five PIs. The local PSI CSV lists 129 distinct test labels across six PIs, and the TM appendix has six PI-specific matrices. These source statements conflict. The materials reviewed do **not** establish whether the three-row difference reflects a different counting convention, repeated conditions, exclusions, missing source rows, or document versions. No explanation is selected. The PI/test identifiers have not been fully crosswalked from the PSI CSV to every Appendix A row; the report's “126 tests” may refer to a scope not stated in the CSV, but that is only an **INFERENCE**, not a resolution.

**Variables and evidence needed.** NASA/TM printed p. 5 identifies air-velocity display in cm/s and fan voltage display as 10× voltage; p. 6 describes O2/CO2/CO sensing. Multi-value display strings still need segmentation and calibration context. The PSI CSV lacks row-level pressure and ambient temperature, so study-level nominal values must not be copied into rows. Reconcile the counts using a versioned crosswalk with columns for PSI `PI`/`Test #`, Appendix A PI/table/row, inclusion/exclusion reason, repeat-condition status, and source version. If no source explains three records, ask NASA PSI/source owners; until then retain both 126 and 129 with their source citations.

## PSI-98 — SAFFIRE-I

The CSV has two sample rows (S1/S2) and two F1/F2 camera/phase annotations, not four independent test points. The two samples include reported material, dimensions, nominal flow, O2 estimates, ignition values, burn time, direction, and camera references. The PSI-98 metadata `Comment[Approach]` and `SAFFIRE-I and SAFFIRE-III DoD.docx` opening text/Table 1 support protocol-level context and distinguish sample from camera rows. The table's 40.6 cm length/94 cm width labels conflict with narrative dimensions/orientation; preserve them as written (**UNVERIFIED**). O2 entries are approximate/ranges; burn time is not a flame-spread rate or a verified outcome.

`SAFFIRE-1_MergeResult-jpeg_Update_Final_v2.xlsx` is an image-file index (IDs, names, timestamps/status), not JPEG measurements. The DoD says sensor workbooks and image archives are separate experiment-data attachments. Neither is in the local repository. Images/video are needed to assess time-resolved flame behavior where the CSV has no outcome label; calibrated sensor files are needed for O2/pressure/temperature time series. Without them, nominal settings and metadata remain study-level or table-level evidence only.

## PSI-99 — SAFFIRE-II

The CSV has nine sample IDs (`2-1`–`2-9`) plus six annotations/notes. The nine rows combine multiple materials/settings and reported burn/spread quantities. `SAFFIRE-II DoD.docx`, opening text/Table 1, supports the nine-sample-card organization. Table Note 1 describes the estimated O2 basis; this is a derived estimate and must not be treated as a direct O2 sensor value. `?-g` remains an unresolved literal field, not a synonym for microgravity. Keep the explicit `1-g` comparator separate. `037 mm` stays malformed/ambiguous; do not add a decimal. Two material fields are blank. Burn length, spread length, rate, damage length, and outcomes are distinct quantities.

`SAFFIRE-II_MergeResult-jpeg_v5_Final.xlsx` is an image index, not image or sensor payload. The local PSI-99 folder lacks the source data files and science/engineering documents needed to inspect the reported measurement derivations. Images can help classify temporal outcomes; they cannot substitute for calibrated gas/pressure sensors. No row-level outcome is currently verified.

## PSI-100 — SAFFIRE-III

The CSV contains two sample rows S1/S2 describing SIBAL sample/configuration, nominal 30 cm/s flow and concurrent/opposed directions. This supports table-level setup, not time-resolved speed, burn result, or independent replicate count. `SAFFIRE-I and SAFFIRE-III DoD.docx` and the PSI-100 metadata support broad protocol and sensor context; length/width orientation still conflicts and units embedded in table cells require careful parsing.

The JPEG and RAW `MergeResult` workbooks are indexes. The RAW index contains acquisition/file metadata, not physical sensor values. The DoD states sensor Excel files and original camera data are separate files. They are absent from local `raw_data/`. Image sequences are needed to verify flame behavior; sensor payloads are necessary for measured O2, pressure, temperature, and other instrument channels. The table's setup fields remain usable as source descriptions but are not a verified outcome dataset.

## Cross-investigation conclusions

- **VERIFIED:** The local harmonised table is header-only; there are zero records meeting the repository's stated row verification rule. This does not mean NASA performed zero experiments.
- **VERIFIED:** The five experimental CSVs have 263 candidate table rows under the initial row-role rules; the primary BASS matrix adds nine unmatched keyed rows. Those are source-row counts only.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Number of independent experiments, outcome class counts, run-resolved comparable O2/pressure/flow values, and gravity for SAFFIRE-II `?-g`.
- **MISSING:** SAFFIRE original image and sensor payloads, and BASS/BASS-II run-level data necessary for the open row crosswalks.
- Outcomes must remain `unknown` when absent and `ambiguous` when evidence exists without a defensible label definition. A note describing extinction, a completed burn, or ignition is not automatically transferable across apparatuses.
- Neither microgravity nor 1 g data justify lunar/partial-gravity extrapolation. Do not convert between distinct gravity conditions using a numeric interpolation.

## Required next evidence

1. A BASS data-owner crosswalk for the three IDs, nine extra matrix rows, oxygen scale, pressure basis, and fan/air display calibration.
2. A BASS-II PSI-to-Appendix A crosswalk and explanation/version provenance for 126 vs 129.
3. PSI experiment-data attachments for SAFFIRE sensor files and camera payloads, with checksums and source records.
4. A shared outcome codebook that cites the source definition and allows unknown/ambiguous values; second-person verification for each promoted row.
